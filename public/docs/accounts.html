Accounts

Accounts & projects

With an account, wireframes live on the server, grouped in projects, available on every device — and optionally to your coding agents.

Signing in is optional

If the site runs the accounts backend you see a sign-in screen; Continue without an account keeps the guest editor. Sign-up needs an email confirmation; password reset works by email. On a purely static host there is no backend and Wirefragma is always the guest editor.

The projects panel

  • Projects are folders; wireframes are the rows inside, with a compact age (“5m”, “2h”).
  • + in the header creates a project (with a first wireframe); + on a project row adds a wireframe.
  • … on a row: rename (or double-click), duplicate, delete (with confirmation); on a project also Export project (.wfproj).
  • Import at the bottom adds an exported Markdown/JSON as a new wireframe in a project you choose — or a whole .wfproj as a new project.
  • The panel collapses to a narrow rail; switching wireframes is instant and keeps each one's undo history.

Autosave and conflicts

Edits save about 0.7 s after you stop. Each save says which revision it was based on; if the wireframe changed in the meantime (another tab, another device, an agent), nothing is overwritten and a banner offers:

  • Keep my version — save yours over the newer one;
  • Load the other version — discard your unsaved edits and load the latest copy.

The footer of the panel shows the save state: saved, saving, unsaved changes, offline (retrying every 5 s) or changed elsewhere.

Changes made elsewhere

While the tab is visible, Wirefragma checks every few seconds (and whenever you return to the tab) whether anything changed:

  • an open wireframe without unsaved edits is reloaded, with a short Updated elsewhere notice;
  • if you do have unsaved edits, you get the conflict banner above — your work is never replaced silently;
  • new, renamed, duplicated or deleted projects and wireframes appear in the panel; if the open wireframe was deleted, another one opens.

This is how edits from an MCP agent show up while you watch.

Settings

Avatar menu → Settings…: display name, language and theme (stored with the account), password, MCP access (tokens for coding agents) and account deletion. Deleting the account removes every project, wireframe and token.

Privacy and security

  • Passwords are hashed; sessions use an HttpOnly cookie; every change is protected against cross-site requests.
  • Your wireframes are only readable with your session, or with an MCP token you created.
  • Wirefragma sends your wireframes nowhere and calls no AI service.
